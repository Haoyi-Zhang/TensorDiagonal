# Third-party material

`proofs/jmlr2e.sty` is the unmodified JMLR style supplied with the project template. Its header, contributor history, source address and all internal text are retained. It is not covered by this repository's MIT text. Upstream: https://github.com/JmlrOrg/jmlr-style-file . A separate explicit license grant was not present in the supplied style file; no broader grant is asserted here. The article also uses that same unmodified supplied file.

The manuscript and proof bibliography attribute scholarly results. Full scholarly PDFs, upstream research code, datasets, solvers and benchmark implementations are not redistributed. All experiment tensors, witnesses, test oracles and plots are original synthetic materials generated by this repository. Python and LaTeX are execution dependencies, not bundled distributions. The public arXiv identifiers and publisher URLs in `external_resources.csv` identify consulted works; online scholarly records can evolve, so offline reproduction here covers the exact experiments and proofs provided, not a frozen web crawl.
